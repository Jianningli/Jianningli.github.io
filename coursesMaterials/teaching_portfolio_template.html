01 Teaching Philosophy & Methodology
My instructional approach focuses on building rigorous methodological foundations in machine learning and data science, complemented by practical applications in digital medicine and healthcare. Teaching across computer science and clinical faculties at Free University of Berlin and Charité, I instruct students with diverse backgrounds ranging from computer science to clinical medicine. Coursework and seminars emphasize algorithm fundamentals, reproducible code implementation, statistical validation, and translational deployment of AI for clinical decision support and 3D medical imaging.
Prospective Student Supervision: I regularly supervise Bachelor's and Master's theses in 3D medical shape reconstruction, generative modeling, Bayesian neural networks, and clinical decision support.
